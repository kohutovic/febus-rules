# Referee Quick Reference

> **Note:** Informative summary for referees. The full rules prevail.

## Commands

'On guard!' → 'Are you ready?' → 'Fence!' → … → 'Halt!' (see G.7–G.8)

## Stop the exchange ('Halt!') when

a valid hit lands · an invalid hit complicates further evaluation · a fencer
leaves the arena with both feet · end of time · dangerous or confused fencing ·
equipment failure · injury · a fencer requests a break (raised arm) (see G.8)

## Arena boundaries

- Both feet out → stop immediately; annul everything after the crossing (G.13–G.14).
- One foot out → a hit still scores if the action started before 'Halt!' (G.15).
- Both feet out without a prior valid hit (penalty 0.3): first time a verbal
  warning, then a point against (G.16).
- Pushed out accidentally by the opponent → no penalty (G.17).

## Bout parameters (defaults)

Pools: 2 min or 5 points (O.17). Direct elimination: 2 × 2 min (1 min rest) or 7
points (O.28, O.31). Tie → 1 min sudden death; before it starts, the Referee
draws lots for priority in case nobody scores (G.20; see also O.19, O.33).
Medal bouts: the Organising Team may raise the point limit (e.g. 10 or 15) and
adjust the time structure (O.29).

## Breaks

Equipment failure: up to 3 min (G.23). Injury/cramp: up to 10 min (confirmed by
medical staff; once per injury per bout) (G.24).

## Cards

Group 1: **YELLOW** → **RED** → **RED** · Group 2: **RED** every time ·
Group 3: **RED** → **BLACK** (3.1, 3.2) / **YELLOW** → **BLACK** (3.3, 3.4) ·
Group 4: **BLACK** immediately.
