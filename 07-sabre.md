# Sabre competitions

## Target Area

**S.1** Targets are restricted from the hips up, including arms and head, but with legs being off-target.

**S.2** Strikes to an off-target area do not score, do not stop the exchange, and do not assign any
   points.

**S.3** Repeated, deliberate strikes to an off-target area are judged as strikes to a forbidden target and dealt with accordingly.

## Sabre-specific scoring considerations

**S.4** **Simultaneous hits** - When both fencers hit simultaneously, the score does not change.

**S.5** **Hits through blade contact** - Hits landed with blade contact (through a parry or over the cross-guard) are valid only if the hit retains meaningful force.

**S.6** **Point-in-line exception** - If the defender holds their point outstretched and the opponent runs into the point, the distance traversed ceases to play a role in judging validity.

**S.7** **Dominance actions** - Note that the general dominance rule (see [G.34–G.37](03-general.md)) applies only to weapon locks and constraining actions, not grappling, as grappling is forbidden in sabre.

## Forbidden actions

**S.8** Grappling or close quarter combat is forbidden; it belongs to the first group of offences (see
   penalty 1.5).

**S.9** Engaging in grappling, close quarters combat, or corps-a-corps is immediately stopped by the Referee.

## Priority System

Sabre uses the standard priority (right of way, historically *Vor*) system. See [Right of Way and Priority Rules](04-right-of-way.md) for detailed priority conventions.
